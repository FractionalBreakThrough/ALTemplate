FAQ

Still have questions?

? Frequently Asked Questions
Does it affect sound quality or playback?

Nope. The thickness and flatness are designed so it won't interfere with tracking, sound quality, or record wear. In 99% of cases, there's nothing to adjust.

Does it work with all turntables?

Vinylessence™ is compatible with the vast majority of turntable brands including Audio-Technica, Sony, Victrola, Crosley, Numark, Technics, Pioneer, and more. As long as your turntable uses a standard 11–12″ slipmat and doesn't have an unusually short spindle.

Is it safe for my vinyl?

Yes. The top surface is smooth anti-static acrylic, just like any other acrylic slipmat. All internal electronics are fully enclosed and protected.

How long does the battery last?

Typical runtimes are 13–16 hours per charge on the 4000 mAh cell, depending on colour mode and brightness.

When does it ship?

Pre-orders ship as part of the first production run. First shipments are expected to start as early as August 2026.
