What Equipping Hour Is

Equipping Hour is designed to provide our fellowship with a biblical Christian education in an interactive setting — for the purpose of creating intelligent, thoughtful, warmhearted Christians who wear Christ attractively.

"Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ." Colossians 1:28

Two Tracks

Adult and Youth, side by side.

When Equipping Hour is in session, both classes meet at the same time so families can come together and split into age-appropriate teaching.

Adult Equipping Hour

For grown-ups · Open to all

Topical, interactive teaching for adults at every stage — long-time believers, brand-new Christians, and the spiritually curious. Past topics have ranged from how to read the Bible, to apologetics, to walking with God in suffering, to parenting and marriage from a biblical lens.

Each class is open and can be joined at any time — no homework, no pressure, no embarrassment about not knowing the answer. Discussion is encouraged.

On Break Stay tuned for the next class

Youth Equipping Hour

For students · Middle & High School

The same heart, scaled for students. Real questions, real answers, and a space where teenagers can wrestle with the Bible without the pressure of having it all figured out. Teaching aimed at building a thoughtful Christian worldview before they leave home.

Open to any 6th–12th grader. Friends always welcome — bring one.

Our Goals

Three things every class aims for.

When we sit down to plan a class, here's what we're after.

01

Topical instruction

Real teaching on issues of specific relevance — drawn from Scripture, applied to the questions you're actually asking.

02

A bridge to deeper relationships

Class is the entry point — community is the destination. Friendships formed in Equipping Hour often outlast the class itself.

03

Christ on display

So that Christ would be seen in our families, our church, and our interaction with the world — not as a slogan, but as a way of life.
