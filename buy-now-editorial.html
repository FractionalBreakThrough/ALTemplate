The Object

Eight and a half millimetres of slipmat. Acrylic surface, anti-static, polished to play as quietly as anything else on your platter. A USB-C port sits flush with the edge. A 4000 mAh cell does the rest.

Inside, an even bed of LEDs diffuses through the layers and out through the record above. The colour, the brightness, the pattern — all of it lives on your phone. The slipmat itself stays quiet, until you don't want it to.

Questions

Does it affect sound quality or playback?

No. The thickness and flatness are designed so it won't interfere with tracking, sound quality, or record wear. In 99% of cases, there's nothing to adjust.

Does it work with all turntables?

Vinylessence™ fits the vast majority of decks that accept a standard 11–12″ slipmat — Audio-Technica, Sony, Victrola, Crosley, Numark, Technics, Pioneer, and more. Unusually short spindles are the exception.

Is it safe for my vinyl?

Yes. The top surface is smooth anti-static acrylic, just like any other acrylic slipmat. All internal electronics are fully enclosed.

How long does the battery last?

Typical runtimes are 13–16 hours per charge on the 4000 mAh cell, depending on color mode and brightness.

When does it ship?

Pre-orders ship as part of the first production run. First shipments are expected to start as early as August 2026.
