Who We Are

A Community
on a Mission

We are a community of believers in Temecula, CA, dedicated to knowing Jesus and making Him known. Our church is more than a building; it's a family where everyone is welcome.

Whether you have been a Christian for years or are just exploring faith, there is a place for you here at Temecula Hills.

First Time?

What to expect
on your first visit

We know visiting a new church can be intimidating. We want to make your first experience at Temecula Hills as seamless as possible. Here's what you can expect.

1

Arrive

Our parking team will direct you to a spot. Look for the orange "New Here" signs near the entrance.

2

Kids Check-In

Safe, fun, and biblical teaching for your children. Check-in takes about 5 minutes — we'll print a matching tag so only you can pick them up.

3

Grab Coffee

Stop by our hospitality tent for free coffee and donuts before service. Plenty of time to settle in or meet someone new.

4

Worship

Service lasts about 75 minutes with contemporary worship and biblical preaching. Same flow at the 8:45 and 10:30 services.
