Find Your Kid's Class

Four classes, every Sunday.

Each class is led by background-checked volunteers and follows our age-appropriate Bible curriculum.

Crawlers
0–2 Years

A bright, calm space staffed by trained nursery caregivers. Cribs, toys, snacks, and a check-in system that texts you if your little one needs you.

What to bring Diapers, wipes, a labeled bottle or sippy, and any comfort item. A favorite blanket or stuffed friend goes a long way.
Campers
2–5 Years

Songs, simple Bible stories, hands-on crafts, and lots of laughter. A whole hour designed around the way little ones actually learn — through play and repetition.

What to expect A short story, a craft to take home, a snack, and lots of moving around. Pickup is at the same upstairs door you dropped off.
Climbers
K–Grade 2

Bible-based lessons that meet kids where they are — worship, a teaching time, small group circles, and a memory verse to take home each week.

What to bring A Bible if you have one (we'll loan one if not). Curiosity and a willingness to participate are all that's actually required.
Conquerors
Grades 3–5

The big-kid class. Deeper Bible teaching, real conversation, and small groups that take questions seriously — because kids this age have real ones.

What to expect Worship, a 15-minute teaching, and small group time with peers and a leader who knows them by name.

Our Approach

Five things every lesson aims for.

Drawn from Deuteronomy 6:1–9. We partner with parents, family members, volunteers, and the church body to keep these woven through everything we do.

1

Relationship

Relationships are the foundation of our children's ministry. Ministry to children begins with the volunteer's heart — as they love God, that love flows through them to the kids.

2

Experience

Teaching is intertwined with what children experience every day. We design activities that take learning past a cognitive exercise and into an actual, felt experience.

3

Bible Truth

Our ministry focuses on teaching the Bible to children. Scripture is most effectively taught when the Bible itself is studied, read, and taught — and we strive to develop a deep love for God's Word.

4

Discussion

We guide children in connecting Scripture's truth to their everyday lives through small group discussions. The Bible isn't just a story — it has real-life application to them.

5

Response

We give kids practical opportunities to respond to the Word of God. The Word should affect what a child does with their hands, how they think, and how they treat others — no child should leave church without knowing how to respond.

For Parents

A few important things to know.

If it's your first Sunday with us, here's what to expect at check-in.

Check-in & Pick-up

  • Fill out a registration card for each of your children if you haven't yet — once it's done, you're set for every Sunday after.
  • Someone must check in and pick up their child from class. No student will be dismissed without a designated pick-up person — full stop.
  • We strongly recommend a bathroom visit before class. If your child needs a bathroom trip mid-service, you'll be paged using the buzzer you receive at check-in.
  • Arrive a few minutes early on your first Sunday — we'll show you where to go and walk you through the matching-tag system so only you can pick your child up.

Special Needs & Allergies

  • Have a child with special needs? Mention it on the registration card or note it on the contact form below — we'll have a buddy ready and a quiet space available if you'd like one.
  • Note allergies and dietary restrictions on the registration card. Snacks are nut-free by default.
  • Worried about something specific? Reach out before Sunday — we'd rather over-prepare than have you feel uncertain.
  • Questions about Children's Ministry? Reach our children's pastor Alex Bruda at abruda@egmworld.org.
