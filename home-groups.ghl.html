HOME GROUPS

Real life happens
around a table.

Small groups meeting in homes throughout the Temecula-Murrieta Valley. We gather, eat, open the Bible, and pray for one another. Everyone's welcome — bring your story, bring a friend.

Find a Group Get in Touch
Why Home Groups

At Temecula Hills, our purpose is to glorify God by making maturing disciples of Jesus Christ. One of the primary ways this happens is by enjoying biblical community with one another — in homes, around tables, in real relationships that move beyond the superficial.

"And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together, as is the habit of some, but encouraging one another." Hebrews 10:24–25
How It Works

Four things every group does.

Different homes, different days, different studies — but the rhythm is the same.

Gather

10–15 people in a living room every week. Some weeks you'll have lots to say. Some weeks you'll just listen. Both are good.

Eat

Some groups share a full meal. Some just bring snacks. The food matters less than the table.

Study

We open the Bible together — usually a book of Scripture or a topical study. No experience needed.

Pray

Real prayers, for real things — kids, marriages, jobs, illness, joy. We carry each other in the in-between.
