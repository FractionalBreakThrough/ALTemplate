Your Sunday, Step by Step

What to expect when you visit

From the parking lot to the closing song, here's how Sunday morning flows.

01

Arrive & Park

Pull into our main lot and look for the orange "New Here" signs near the entrance. Our parking team will wave you to a guest spot up front.

02

Get Welcomed

Stop by the Welcome Tent just inside the front doors. We'll get you a name tag, hand you a gift, and walk you wherever you need to go.

03

Grab a Coffee

Free coffee, tea, and donuts in the lobby. Plenty of time before service to settle in, meet someone new, or just sit and breathe.

04

Worship Together

Service is about 75 minutes — a few worship songs, an announcement or two, and a Bible-based message you can actually take home.

The Most Asked Question

"What should I wear?"

Honestly — whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see jeans and t-shirts, sundresses, button-downs, and the occasional cowboy boot. There's no dress code. Just come.

For Your Family

A safe, fun place for every age

Every kids' room is staffed by background-checked volunteers. Check-in takes about 5 minutes — we'll print you a matching tag so only you can pick them up.

Ages 0–3

Nursery

A bright, calm space staffed by trained caregivers. Cribs, snacks, and a check-in system that texts you if your little one needs you.

Ages 4–11

Elementary

Bible-based lessons that meet kids where they are — with worship, games, small group time, and a memory verse to take home.

Ages 12–18

Youth

Middle and high schoolers meet Sundays during service plus Wednesdays at 7 PM for worship, teaching, and small groups.

Have a child with special needs?

Mention it on the visit form below or call us ahead — we'll have a buddy ready and a quiet space available if you'd like one.

75 Minutes

What happens during the service

Roughly how Sunday morning unfolds. Nothing weird, nothing unexpected — just worship, the Bible, and time to respond.

9:30 AM

Doors Open

Coffee, donuts, and a chance to meet someone before service. Kids' check-in opens at the same time.

10:00 AM

Worship Begins

3–4 contemporary worship songs led by our band. Sing along, sit, stand — whatever feels right.

10:15 AM

Welcome & Announcements

A quick hello, a few things happening this week, and a moment to greet the people around you.

10:25 AM

Bible Teaching

A 30–35 minute message from one of our pastors, walking through a passage of Scripture and applying it to real life.

11:00 AM

Closing & Response

A final song and a chance to respond — whether that's prayer, communion (first Sunday of the month), or just sitting in stillness.

Still Wondering?

Common questions

Where do I park?
Pull into the main lot off Church Road. Look for the orange "New Here" signs — our parking team will wave you to a guest spot up front. There's accessible parking right by the main entrance.
How long is the service?
About 75 minutes — start to finish. Worship is around 20 minutes, the message is 30–35 minutes, and the rest is welcome, announcements, and a closing song.
Do you celebrate communion?
Yes — on the first Sunday of every month. It's open to anyone who follows Jesus, and we'll walk you through what to do if it's your first time.
Is there a dress code?
Nope. Come in whatever you're comfortable in — jeans, dresses, shorts, suits. You'll see all of it.
What if I have questions about faith?
Bring them. Stop by the Welcome Tent after service or use the form above to let us know — a pastor or Connect team member will follow up personally.
Can I just observe without being singled out?
Absolutely. We won't ask visitors to stand up, raise hands, or fill anything out unless you want to. Sit, listen, leave — no pressure.
Is the building accessible?
Yes. Step-free entry from the parking lot, accessible restrooms, and assistive listening available at the sound booth. Mention any specific needs on the visit form and we'll have everything ready.
