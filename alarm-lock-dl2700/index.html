    Technical Specifications

    Operation & Handing

    Clutch: Free-turning outer lever prevents vandalism. Instant inside egress.

    Keypad: Vandal-proof all-metal. Adjustable entry time (5-20s). Mute capable.

    Handing: Non-handed (fully reversible).

    Door Specs

    Prep: Standard ANSI 161 Series (with added through-bolt holes).

    Thickness: 1-5/8" to 1-7/8" standard.

    Backset: 2-3/4" standard (2-3/8" optional).

    Hardware & Power

    Latchbolt: 1/2" throw, solid brass deadlocking. T-strike & ASA included.

    Power: 5 AA alkaline batteries (200k cycles). Low battery alert.

    Motor: Unique heavy-duty Flex Drive® motor.

    Cylinders & Keying

    Cylinder: Solid brass 6-pin (SC1 standard). Accepts 6/7-pin IC Cores.

    IC Core Prep: Models available for Yale, Medeco, Sargent, Corbin/Russwin, Schlage.
