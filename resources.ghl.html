RightNow Media promo video

At Temecula Hills, our heart is to bring glory to God by making maturing disciples of Jesus. To grow as disciples of Christ, we need to grow deep in understanding and being transformed by His Word.

Because of this, we are inviting you to have a free account to an exciting video library called RightNow Media. It’s essentially the “Netflix of Video Bible Studies” — you will have instant access to thousands of great videos for kids, youth, parents, married couples, and more. You can view these videos from your phone, tablet, or computer. You can also view them in a group setting through a Smart TV using Roku, Amazon FireTV, Google Chromecast, or Apple TV. Just search for the RightNow Media app and sign in.

We hope you enjoy this as our free gift to you and that it helps you grow as a disciple of Jesus!

General Interest

Ministries and websites we’ve found trustworthy and edifying over the years.

  • Reasonable Faith Intelligent, articulate, and gracious Christian perspective on the most important issues concerning the truth of the Christian faith — the ministry of William Lane Craig.
  • The Gospel Coalition A network of evangelical churches that seeks to serve the local church by providing gospel-centered and Christ-focused content.
  • Desiring God God-centered resources from the ministry of John Piper. In particular, Piper’s biographies of Christians in history are excellent.
  • Grace To You The ministry of John MacArthur.
  • Walking in the Promises The ministry of Marcelo Tolopilo.
  • Global Church Ministries Ministry of our longtime friend, Craig Poston.
  • Collaborative Development International Ministry of our longtime ministry partner, Rob Foster.
