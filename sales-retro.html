? Frequently Asked Questions
Does it affect sound quality or playback?
Nope! The thickness and flatness are designed so it won't interfere with tracking, sound quality, or record wear. In 99% of cases, there's nothing to adjust.
Does it work with all turntables?
Vinylessence™ fits the vast majority of decks that accept a standard 11–12″ slipmat — Audio-Technica, Sony, Victrola, Crosley, Numark, Technics, Pioneer, and more.
Is it safe for my vinyl?
Yes. The top surface is smooth anti-static acrylic, just like any other acrylic slipmat. All internal electronics are fully enclosed.
How long does the battery last?
Typical runtimes are 13–16 hours per charge on the 4000mAh cell, depending on color mode and brightness.
When does it ship?
Pre-orders ship as part of our first production run. First shipments are expected to start as early as August 2026.
