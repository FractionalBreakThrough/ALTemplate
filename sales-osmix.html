F1 Does it affect sound quality or playback?
> Nope. The thickness and flatness are designed so it won't interfere with tracking, sound quality, or record wear. In 99% of cases, there's nothing to adjust.
F2 Does it work with all turntables?
> Vinylessence(TM) fits the vast majority of decks that accept a standard 11-12" slipmat. Audio-Technica, Sony, Victrola, Crosley, Numark, Technics, Pioneer, and more. Unusually short spindles are the exception.
F3 Is it safe for my vinyl?
> Yes. The top surface is smooth anti-static acrylic, just like any other acrylic slipmat. All internal electronics are fully enclosed.
F4 How long does the battery last?
> Typical runtimes are 13-16 hours per charge on the 4000 mAh cell, depending on color mode and brightness.
F5 When does it ship?
> Pre-orders ship as part of the first production run. First shipments are expected to start as early as August 2026.
