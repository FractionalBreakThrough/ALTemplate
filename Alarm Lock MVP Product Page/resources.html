💡 Pro Tips

Get the Most Out of Every Session

Small habits that lead to dramatically better results from your AI creative team.

🎯

Be Specific With Your Briefs

The more context you give, the better the output. Include your target audience, desired tone, key messages, and any constraints. A detailed brief saves rounds of revision.

🔁

Iterate, Don't Start Over

If the first output isn't perfect, refine it. Tell the agent what to change — "make it shorter," "more casual," "add a stat." Each round gets closer to exactly what you need.

🤝

Let Agents Collaborate

Use the Strategist to build a brief, hand it to the Copywriter for execution, then pass the draft to the Creative Director for review. The agents work best as a team.

📚

Save Your Best Outputs

When you get a result you love, save it. Build a library of your best work to use as reference for future sessions. It helps the agents learn your preferences faster.
