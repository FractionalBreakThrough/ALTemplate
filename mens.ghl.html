Our Heart for Men

Our heart for the men of Temecula Hills is simple — that we'd be men of Scripture, of prayer, and of one another. Men who carry their families with grit and grace, who don't do faith alone, and who keep showing up for each other when life gets heavy.

Monthly Men's Breakfast

One Saturday a month · 8:00 AM

Monthly · Saturday mornings

Our monthly rhythm. Eggs, pancakes, coffee, and a 20-minute message from one of our pastors or a guest. The food's good, the conversation's better.

No prior commitment, no awkward icebreakers — just walk in, grab a plate, and find a seat. Bring your sons. Bring a neighbor.
