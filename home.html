The Problem

Creatives Were Being Left Behind
by the AI Revolution

We saw it happening in real time. Every week, a new AI tool launched promising to "revolutionize creativity." But when our team—art directors, copywriters, strategists—actually tried them, the same problems kept surfacing:

😶

Generic Outputs

Every AI gave the same bland, corporate-speak results. Nothing that sounded like a real creative professional wrote it.

🧠

No Creative Context

Tools built by engineers for engineers. They didn't understand briefs, brand guidelines, or the messy reality of client work.

💔

Creative Burnout

Creatives were drowning in busywork—research, revisions, admin. The parts that kill inspiration were growing, not shrinking.

The Moment

So We Built What We Wished Existed

"We didn't set out to build an AI company. We're an ad agency. But after months of trying every tool on the market and watching our team struggle with outputs that felt like they came from a different planet—we realized nobody was building AI for people like us."

— Carpe Diem Creative, Las Vegas

Step One

We trained AI on how creatives actually think

Not prompt engineering tricks. Real creative frameworks—how a strategist builds a brief, how a copywriter finds voice, how an art director gives feedback.

Step Two

We gave each agent a distinct personality and specialty

Not one chatbot that does everything poorly. A full department—strategist, writer, designer, auditor, researcher—each with deep expertise in their role.

Step Three

We made them collaborate—with you and each other

The agents hand work off between each other. Arthur audits your brand, Max builds a strategy, Paige writes the copy, Crystal gives creative direction. A real workflow, not a chat window.
