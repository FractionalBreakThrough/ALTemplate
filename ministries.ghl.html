Home Groups

Real life happens around a table.

At Temecula Hills, our purpose is to glorify God by making maturing disciples of Jesus Christ. One of the primary ways this happens is by enjoying biblical community with one another through Home Groups — small, home-based groups that gather throughout the Temecula-Murrieta Valley where people meet together, eat together, discuss God's Word together, and do life together.

The Bible tells us that as a church, we are a family — members of one another. To truly live like a family, we need real, deep relationships that move beyond the superficial, so that we are devoted to one another as we encourage one another to follow Christ.

Please consider joining one of our Home Groups so we can be mutually encouraged and pursue God's mission for our church together!

Our Mission

Helping children grow as maturing disciples of Jesus.

"…if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you." Matthew 17:20

We welcome all elementary-aged kids and younger to join our children's ministry each Sunday for both services — 8:45 AM and 10:30 AM in our upstairs classroom gathering space. Mountain Mover Kids includes children from birth through 5th grade, and exists to bring glory to God by helping kids grow as maturing disciples of Jesus.

Our Objectives

Five things every lesson aims for.

Drawn from Deuteronomy 6:1–9. We partner with parents, family members, volunteers, and the church body to keep these woven through everything we do.

1 · Relationship

Relationships are the foundation of our children's ministry. Ministry to children begins with the children's worker's heart — as they love God, that love flows through them to the kids.

2 · Experience

Teaching is intertwined with what children experience every day. We design activities that take learning past a cognitive exercise and into an actual experience — talking and hearing are valuable, but experience has lasting impact.

3 · Bible Truth

Our ministry focuses on teaching the Bible to children. Scripture is most effectively taught when the Bible itself is studied, read, and taught. We strive to develop a deep respect and love for God's Word.

4 · Discussion

We guide children in connecting Scripture's truth to their everyday lives through small group discussions. The Bible isn't just a story — it has real-life application to them, individually and personally.

5 · Response

We give kids practical opportunities to respond to the Word of God in everyday life. The Word should affect what a child does with their hands, how they think, and how they treat others. No child should leave church without knowing how to respond.

Our Values

Four characteristics we develop.

In order to see children grow as maturing disciples of Jesus, our Children's Ministry is shaped by four core commitments.

Emphasize

Our Children's Ministry is led by people committed to the importance of ministry to children. As the head of the church, Jesus placed value and importance on ministry to children — and we follow His lead (Matthew 19:13–15).

Expose

We reach out to expose non-Christian children to the gospel of Jesus Christ. The Father is concerned that no child be lost from a relationship with Him. In word and deed, we share the love of Christ with children in our community.

Equip

We give children the tools to live as followers of Christ and become more and more like Jesus. Christianity isn't fire insurance — it's a new life lived with Him and in Him. We help kids live the life Christ has for them.

Empower

We empower children to minister to others, exercising their God-given gifts. Children are gifted by God for ministry — exercising their faith in service enhances the Kingdom and gives them a greater sense of spiritual vitality.
