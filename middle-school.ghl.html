Loud, real,
and worth it.

Tuesday nights of games, worship, real Bible study, and small groups. Plus camps, overnighters, pool parties, and the occasional pizza emergency. There's a seat with your name on it.

Tuesday Nights Get in Touch
For The Students

Middle school is a wild season — friendships forming, identities testing, faith taking on shape that's actually theirs. We believe these years matter, and that students this age can know Jesus and follow Him just as deeply as anyone older.

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity." 1 Timothy 4:12
Every Tuesday Night

Hangout, worship, Word, small groups.

Middle School Youth Group meets 6:30 – 8:45 PM in our downstairs main gathering space. Here's how the night flows.

6:30 – 7:00 PM

Hangout & Games

Casual time, games, and snacks. The purpose is to create an atmosphere where students connect with peers and leaders. We believe the connections formed during this time are vital in the spiritual journey of our students.

  • Pizza, snacks, and drinks.
  • Group games and free play.
  • First-time? Just walk in — leaders will spot you.
7:00 – 7:45 PM

Worship & Bible Study

A serious time of worship and Bible study. Students are challenged to look into the Bible for themselves and take notes. This time introduces the topic discussed later in small groups.

Bring with youA Bible, a journal, and a pen — every week. We'll loan you a Bible if you don't have one.
7:45 – 8:45 PM

Small Groups

Students break into small, gender-specific groups led by an adult to discuss the passage or topic introduced. Real conversation, real questions, and prayer for one another.

  • Same group, same leader, every week.
  • A safe place to ask anything.
  • Pickup at 8:45 PM at the downstairs main entrance.
