Wireless Technology

Why 900MHz Beats Wi-Fi

Standard 2.4GHz Wi-Fi struggles to penetrate commercial building materials. The Trilogy Networx 900MHz transceiver cuts through reinforced concrete, steel studs, and elevator shafts — delivering reliable communication up to 900 feet in clear field.

Superior Penetration

Lower frequency signals pass through concrete, steel, and brick far better than 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi.

No IT Network Dependency

The 900MHz mesh operates independently of your building's Wi-Fi. No IP conflicts, no firewall rules, no IT friction.

Power-Saver Radio

Advanced dormancy circuit manages radio sleep cycles, achieving up to 500,000 cycles on standard C-cell batteries.

Decentralized Intelligence

Every Lock Thinks for Itself

Each Trilogy Networx lock stores all programming, schedules, and user data locally. If the network goes down, every door keeps working — and buffers up to 40,000 events until connectivity restores.

Local Logic Engine

All access rules, time schedules, and user credentials are stored on the lock's internal board. No cloud dependency for door decisions.

40,000 Event Buffer

Every access event is logged locally with time/date stamps. When the network reconnects, the full audit trail auto-syncs to the cloud.

Auto Cloud Sync

Schedule changes, firmware updates, and new credentials pushed from MVP are automatically applied when the lock checks in wirelessly.
