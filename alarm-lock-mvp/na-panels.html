Wireless vs Hardwired

When Should You Go Hardwired?

Trilogy Networx wireless locks cover most doors. But some openings need hardwired panels. Here's when to specify an NA-Series controller.

Mag-Lock Doors

Electromagnetic locks need continuous power and supervised wiring. NA panels deliver 12/24V output, door-position monitoring, and request-to-exit inputs.

Electric Strikes

Fail-safe and fail-secure electric strikes require relay-switched power and fire-alarm integration that only a hardwired panel provides.

Elevators & Gates

The CA-2 controller manages elevator floor-select relays and gate operators — openings that have no door leaf for a wireless lock.

Turnstiles & Barriers

Full-height and waist-high turnstiles, optical barriers, and mantrap interlocks — all require relay I/O that wireless locks can't supply.

Fire-Alarm Tie-In

NA panels have supervised fire-alarm inputs. When the FACP triggers, the panel releases all mag-locks for emergency egress — code-mandated behavior.

Auxiliary I/O Integration

Need to trigger a camera preset, activate a strobe, unlock a secondary door, or interlock two openings? NA panels provide the relay outputs and supervised inputs.

NA-1 Single-Door Controller

The entry-level hardwired panel

The NA-1 is the smallest footprint in the NA-Series — purpose-built for standalone mag-lock doors, remote gates, and individual perimeter openings where you need one reader, one lock relay, and full MVP cloud control.

Compact Enclosure

Mounts in a single-gang backbox, door frame, or above-ceiling. Minimal closet space required.

PoE Powered

Pull one Ethernet cable for both data and power. No local power supply, no electrician.

Supervised Inputs

Door contact and REX are end-of-line supervised — tamper and fault conditions reported to MVP in real time.

NA-2 Two-Door Controller

The most-specified NA panel

The NA-2 controls two doors from a single panel — perfect for vestibules, mantrap interlocks, paired entries, and buildings where closet space is shared between two openings. Anti-passback logic prevents tailgating between paired doors.

Mantrap / Interlock Logic

Door 1 must close before Door 2 can open. Hardware-enforced interlock for security vestibules.

Anti-Passback

Credential must badge both in and out. Prevents tailgating and ensures accurate occupancy counts.

2 Auxiliary Outputs

Trigger camera presets, strobes, secondary locks, or signage — fully programmable from MVP.

CA-2 Elevator / Gate Controller

Specialized vertical & vehicle access

The CA-2 turns elevator cabs and parking gates into credentialed access points. In elevator mode, the reader controls which floors a user can select. In gate mode, it triggers the gate operator relay on valid credential presentation.

Floor-Select Matrix

Assign floor access per credential. Tenants see only their authorized floors. Visitors restricted to lobby.

Gate Operator Integration

Dry-contact relay triggers barrier arm, swing gate, or roll-up door. Long-range reader support via OSDP.

Fire Service Recall

Supervised fire-alarm input triggers elevator recall to lobby per ASME A17.1 and local fire code.
