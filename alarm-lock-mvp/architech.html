Built The Right Way

Grade 1 Inside, Designer Outside

ArchiTech Exploded View

  • ANSI/BHMA A156.13 Grade 1

    Passes 1,000,000 operating cycles — rated for the heaviest commercial traffic loads.

  • Standard ANSI Prep

    Fits standard 2-1/8" cross-bore and 161 strike prep. Replaces existing mechanical hardware without door modifications.
