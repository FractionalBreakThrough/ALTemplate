The Core Problem

Traditional Training is Broken

Legacy onboarding forces users to stop working, sit through training, memorize steps, and then attempt to apply what they learned days later. Our approach flips the model entirely.

✗ The Old Way

  • Schedule training sessions that pull users away from their work for hours or days
  • Sit through slide decks and video tutorials with no hands-on context
  • Expect users to memorize complex workflows and recall them later under pressure
  • Users get stuck on day one, immediately call tech support for help
  • High support ticket volume during onboarding — your team becomes a help desk
  • Users plateau at beginner level, never advancing beyond the basics
  • Re-training required every time the software updates or a new feature launches
  • Months to reach competency — if users don't give up first
VS

✓ The Guided Way

  • Interactive overlay appears right inside the application — no separate training needed
  • Users learn by doing: step-by-step guidance walks them through real tasks as they build
  • Like a hand-holding experience — curated paths guide each click, field, and decision
  • First-time setup completed independently, without ever contacting support
  • Support call volume drops dramatically within the first month of deployment
  • Progressive guidance elevates users from beginner to confident intermediate operator
  • Guidance updates automatically with your software — always current, never stale
  • Competency in days, not months — users build confidence with every task completed
