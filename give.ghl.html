Common Questions

Giving FAQ

Is my gift tax-deductible?
Yes. Temecula Hills is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit. You'll receive an automated receipt for each gift, and an annual giving statement in January for the prior tax year.
Can I give anonymously?
For accounting and tax-receipt purposes, the church needs to log who gave. But your name isn't shared publicly, and only the bookkeeping team has access to the records.
Can I designate my gift to a specific fund or missionary?
Yes. The online giving form has a fund dropdown — choose Missions, Benevolence, Building, or General. For specific missionary support, email giving@… and we'll set it up.
Can I cancel or change a recurring gift?
Anytime — no need to call us. Log in to your giving account on the processor's site and update or cancel in two clicks. We never lock anyone in.
What if my card was charged in error?
Email giving@… with the date and amount. We'll refund any duplicate or accidental gift right away — no questions asked.
Do I have to be a member or a Christian to give?
Not at all. Anyone can give. We're grateful for every gift, whether it's your first Sunday or your fortieth year.
How do I give appreciated stock or from my IRA?
Email giving@… and we'll send you the church's brokerage transfer details and tax ID. For Qualified Charitable Distributions from an IRA, your custodian can transfer directly to us.
